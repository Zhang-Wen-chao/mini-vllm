```sql
SELECT
    user_id,
    SUM(amount) AS total_amount
FROM orders
WHERE created_at >= '2026-08-01'
  AND created_at <  '2026-09-01'
GROUP BY user_id
ORDER BY total_amount DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

如果 `created_at` 是 `DATE` 类型，也可以写成：

```sql
WHERE created_at BETWEEN '2026-08-01' AND '2026-08-31'
```
