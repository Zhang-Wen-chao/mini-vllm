```sql
SELECT
    user_id,
    SUM(amount) AS total_amount
FROM orders
WHERE created_at >= '2026-08-01'
  AND created_at <  '2026-09-01'
GROUP BY user_id
ORDER BY total_amount DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

说明：使用 `created_at >= '2026-08-01' AND created_at < '2026-09-01'` 可以完整覆盖 2026 年 8 月，并且对时间字段更友好。
